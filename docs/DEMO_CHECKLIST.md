# Pulse demo checklist

1. Open the landing page and use **Explore a sample analysis** first if Wi-Fi or provider latency is uncertain.
2. Show the labeled sample summary, score confidence, transcript evidence, timeline, and prioritized next practice.
3. Return to **Start practicing**, continue as guest, and complete the three onboarding steps.
4. Choose a curated prompt, 1-minute target, preparation off, and audio + video.
5. Confirm the browser permission explanation, device preview, input meter, countdown, recording, finish, and local-safe capture state.
6. Save and analyze. Narrate the real stages: securing media, transcribing, measuring, evaluating, finalizing.
7. On results, click transcript evidence to seek the private recording; then reopen the session from history.
8. Show that dashboard/progress use real stored sessions and never mix the labeled fixtures.
9. Delete the session or just its media from the privacy controls.

Before presenting: verify Vercel/Supabase/OpenAI/Inngest status, allow microphone/camera for the demo origin, keep the sample route open in a second tab, confirm the OpenAI project can access the configured evaluator model, and test projector scaling at 1440 × 900.
